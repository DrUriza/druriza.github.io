TradELATIN program roadmap

Four bounded maturity layers advance through evidence, validation, explicit interfaces, and readiness gates.

This sequence represents technical and scientific dependency, not simultaneous implementation maturity.

Operational for C1–C8

TradELATIN VR1 — OBSERVE

Reproducible acquisition, processing, validation, contracts, and representation of heterogeneous market observations.

Evidence or preparation

  • C1–C8 operational
  • 33 controlled external data endpoints
  • Live and Synthetic / Emulator modes
  • Versioned contracts and Interactive HMI

Next milestones

  • Consolidate C1–C8 evidence
  • Strengthen traceability
  • Improve synchronization and state characterization
  • Develop C9 common architecture

Conditions to advance

  • Reproducible validation
  • Provider provenance
  • Independent validation of C9 adapters
VR1 Workstream

C9 — Blockchain Financial Networks

Planned / In Development

C9 extends VR1 with a network-agnostic framework for transforming blockchain-native financial activity into normalized financial observables through independently scoped ecosystem adapters.

C9 Common ArchitectureEcosystem-Specific AdaptersValidationFuture Network Integrations

VR1 observes and characterizes markets. It does not predict prices, generate trading signals, or execute trades.

Research

TradELATIN VR2 — STRUCTURE

Reproducible mathematical representations of synchronized VR1 observations.

Evidence or preparation

  • Versioned VR1 datasets
  • Normalization criteria
  • Scientific-gap definition
  • Structural baselines

Next milestones

  • Reproducible Φ(X) transformations
  • Candidate structural states
  • Sensitivity and stability evidence
  • Interfaces for future VR3 evaluation

Conditions to advance

  • Stable input contracts
  • Synthetic controls and baselines
  • Documented limitations

VR2 studies mathematical structure. It does not predict prices, issue signals, or execute trades.

Planned

TradELATIN VR3 — PREDICT

Out-of-sample evaluation of predictive information from admissible VR1 and VR2 inputs.

Evidence or preparation

  • Defined targets and horizons
  • Temporal dataset partitions
  • Baseline and rejection criteria

Next milestones

  • Leakage-controlled walk-forward evaluation
  • Calibration and uncertainty evidence
  • Stability reports
  • Versioned supervised outputs

Conditions to advance

  • Validated inputs
  • Strict temporal separation
  • Independent out-of-sample evidence

VR3 evaluates predictive information. A prediction is not an order, and VR3 does not execute trades.

Long-term planned

TradELATIN VR4 — DECIDE & EXECUTE

Constrained decisions and controlled execution research from independently validated VR3 outputs.

Evidence or preparation

  • Decision-policy research
  • Historical replay
  • Synthetic execution

Next milestones

  • Paper trading
  • Controlled sandbox integration
  • Supervised limited deployment

Conditions to advance

  • Authorization and risk controls
  • Reconciliation and audit evidence
  • Monitoring, stop conditions, and kill switch

VR4 is not currently operational. No autonomous live-trading capability is claimed.

In progress

Public foundation and documentation

  • Institutional website redesign
  • Technical documentation and evidence mapping
  • Status and reproducibility communication
