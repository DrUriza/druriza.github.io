Program roadmap overview

TradELATIN program roadmap

Five architectural roles advance through evidence, validation, explicit interfaces and readiness gates. VR5 is a transversal intelligence layer in development.

This sequence represents technical and scientific dependency, not simultaneous implementation maturity.

C1–C8 Implemented

TradELATIN VR1 — OBSERVE

Reproducible acquisition, processing, validation, contracts, and representation of heterogeneous market observations.

Engineering scope / evidence

  • C1–C8 implemented / emulator-replay validated
  • 33 logical endpoints in the existing C1–C8 architecture
  • Live acquisition paths require provider access; synthetic, emulator, and replay validation
  • Versioned contracts and Interactive HMI

Next milestones

  • Consolidate C1–C8 evidence
  • Strengthen traceability
  • Improve synchronization and state characterization
  • Develop C9 common architecture

Conditions to advance

  • Reproducible validation
  • Provider provenance
  • Independent validation of C9 adapters

Conventional implemented

TradELATIN VR2 — STRUCTURE

Reproducible mathematical representations of synchronized VR1 observations.

Engineering scope / evidence

  • CONVENTIONAL structural implementation scope
  • Versioned VR1 datasets
  • Normalization criteria
  • Scientific-gap definition
  • Structural baselines

Next milestones

  • GEOMETRIC / TOPOLOGICAL research and expansion
  • Reproducible Φ(X) transformations
  • Candidate structural states
  • Sensitivity and stability evidence
  • Interfaces for independent VR3 evaluation

Conditions to advance

  • Stable input contracts
  • Synthetic controls and baselines
  • Documented limitations

VR2 studies mathematical structure. It does not predict prices, issue signals, or execute trades.

Predictive component architecture

TradELATIN VR3 — PREDICT

Out-of-sample evaluation of predictive information from admissible VR1 and VR2 inputs.

Engineering scope / evidence

  • Deterministic predictive component architecture
  • Release-specific validation evidence
  • Defined targets and horizons
  • Temporal dataset partitions
  • Baseline and rejection criteria

Next milestones

  • Leakage-controlled walk-forward evaluation
  • Calibration and uncertainty evidence
  • Stability reports
  • Versioned supervised outputs

Conditions to advance

  • Validated inputs
  • Strict temporal separation
  • Independent out-of-sample evidence

VR3 evaluates predictive information. A prediction is not an order, and VR3 does not execute trades.

Component architecture · gated modes

TradELATIN VR4 — DECIDE & EXECUTE

Decision policy, risk controls and execution authorization from independently validated VR3 outputs. SIMULATION, PAPER and gated LIVE are modes inside VR4.

Engineering scope / evidence

  • Decision and execution component architecture
  • Decision policy and authorization boundaries
  • Historical replay
  • Synthetic execution

Next milestones

  • Paper trading
  • Controlled sandbox integration
  • Supervised limited deployment

Conditions to advance

  • Authorization and risk controls
  • Reconciliation and audit evidence
  • Monitoring, stop conditions, and kill switch

VR4 retains execution authority. LIVE is explicitly gated; no real-capital deployment is claimed.

In development

TradELATIN VR5 — INTELLIGENCE & ORCHESTRATION

Supervised interaction, explanation and coordination across VR1–VR4. VR5 does not replace the existing layers or acquire execution authority.

Architecture / development

  • Transversal interaction model
  • Explicit authority and supervision boundaries
  • Traceability and explainability design

Future capabilities

  • OpenAI API, Agents, Tool Calling and MCP
  • LangGraph and Workflow Automation
  • Human-in-the-loop supervision

Conditions to advance

  • Public implementation and validation evidence
  • Bounded tool permissions and human intervention
  • No bypass of VR4 execution authorization
Authority boundary

LLM ≠ Trading Authority

VR5 can query, explain, coordinate and recommend. Execution authority, risk controls and execution-state transitions remain in VR4.

Prediction ≠ Execution Authorization

Human supervision and explicit VR4 authorization are required at sensitive boundaries. An orchestration request cannot bypass limits, permissions or operating-mode gates.
