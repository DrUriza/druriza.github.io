VR2 · STRUCTURE

Mathematical representation of market structure

Research

TradELATIN VR2 investigates reproducible mathematical representations of synchronized VR1 observations to study market geometry, topology, scale, structural regimes, and transitions.

VR1 ObservationsSynchronization and NormalizationMathematical Representation Φ(X)Structural DescriptorsRegimes and Transitions
Screen

Visual exploration of embeddings, topology, persistence, wavelets, and structural transitions.

Processing

Normalization, PCA/SVD, topology, persistent homology, entropy, distances, and multiscale transformations.

Emulator

Reproducible datasets, controlled perturbations, synthetic states, deterministic seeds, and stability experiments.

Integration

Versioned interfaces connecting VR1 contracts with VR2 transformations and research artifacts.

  • PCA and SVD
  • Topological Data Analysis
  • Persistent homology
  • Mapper
  • Wavelet analysis
  • Entropy and complexity
  • Structural regimes
  • Stability and sensitivity

VR2 studies mathematical structure. It does not predict prices, generate trading signals, or execute trades.

VR3 · PREDICT

Predictive research under temporal validation

Planned

TradELATIN VR3 is planned to evaluate whether VR1 observations and VR2 structural representations contain stable, calibrated, and out-of-sample predictive information.

VR1 Observations + VR2 Structural RepresentationsFeature ConstructionTemporally Validated ModelsCalibrated PredictionsUncertainty and Explainability
Screen

Probabilities, horizons, calibration, uncertainty, explainability, and walk-forward performance.

Processing

Feature construction, training, inference, calibration, metrics, leakage controls, and versioned outputs.

Emulator

Historical replay, synthetic scenarios, regime shifts, perturbations, and out-of-sample evaluation.

Integration

Controlled connection of VR1 and VR2 inputs with versioned predictive models and supervised outputs.

  • Feature construction
  • Temporal validation
  • Baseline models
  • Probabilistic prediction
  • Calibration
  • Uncertainty
  • Explainability
  • Model stability

VR3 evaluates predictive information. It does not autonomously make or execute trading decisions.

VR4 · DECIDE & EXECUTE

Controlled decision and execution research

Long-term planned

TradELATIN VR4 is the long-term layer intended to study how validated predictive outputs could become constrained decisions and controlled execution under explicit risk, governance, supervision, and safety boundaries.

Validated VR3 OutputsDecision PolicyRisk and Authorization ControlsPosition SizingExecution PlanningPaper Trading and SimulationAudited Execution
Screen

Decisions, authorized signals, orders, positions, exposure, limits, alerts, traceability, and kill-switch supervision.

Processing

Decision policies, position sizing, portfolio constraints, risk evaluation, exposure control, and execution planning.

Emulator

Paper trading with commissions, spread, slippage, latency, partial fills, liquidity constraints, and adverse scenarios.

Integration

Controlled connection to execution adapters with permissions, reconciliation, idempotency, environment separation, and monitoring.

  • Decision policies
  • Position sizing
  • Risk limits
  • Execution planning
  • Paper trading
  • Order lifecycle
  • Auditability
  • Kill switch

VR4 is not currently operational. No autonomous live-trading capability is claimed.
