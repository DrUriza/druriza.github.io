VR4 · DECIDE & EXECUTE

Controlled decision and execution research

TradELATIN VR4 is the long-term research and engineering layer intended to study how validated predictive outputs could be converted into constrained decisions and controlled execution under explicit risk, governance, supervision, and safety boundaries.

No autonomous live-trading capability is currently claimed.

Layer purpose

From validated output to audited responsibility

No transition to real execution can occur automatically merely because a prediction exists.

Future areas

Decision, risk, execution, and governance

These are long-term study areas, not implemented capabilities.

Decision policies

Entry and exit conditions under explicit authorization.

Position sizing

Exposure limits and portfolio constraints.

Execution planning

Algorithms, order states, and controlled routing.

Market frictions

Transaction costs, spread, slippage, and latency.

Execution uncertainty

Partial fills, rejection, and liquidity constraints.

Reconciliation

Orders, fills, positions, and accounting consistency.

Governance

Permissions, operating modes, and human supervision.

Auditability

Complete reconstruction of decisions and orders.

Emergency controls

Automatic stop conditions and manual kill switch.

Main questions

What must override automation

  • When should a predictive output be rejected?
  • How should uncertainty affect exposure?
  • Which limits must override every automated decision?
  • How should costs, liquidity, latency, and partial fills affect execution?
  • How can every decision and order be reconstructed and audited?
  • Which tests and approvals are required before changing operating modes?
  • Under which conditions must the system stop automatically?
Component organization

Screen / Processing / Emulator / Integration

Screen

VR4 Screen

Supervisory interface for decisions, authorized signals, orders, positions, exposure, limits, execution state, alerts, traceability, and kill-switch controls.

Processing

VR4 Processing

Decision policies, position sizing, portfolio constraints, exposure control, risk evaluation, execution planning, and safeguards.

Emulator

VR4 Emulator

Paper trading and simulation of commissions, spreads, slippage, latency, fills, rejection, liquidity constraints, disconnections, stress, and adverse scenarios.

Integration

VR4 Integration

Controlled connection of VR3 outputs with execution adapters, including permissions, idempotency, reconciliation, sandbox/live separation, observability, and end-to-end testing.

Safety progression

Deployment gates, not commitments

The final stage is neither committed nor available.

  1. Research only
  2. Historical replay
  3. Synthetic execution
  4. Paper trading
  5. Controlled sandbox integration
  6. Supervised limited deployment
  7. Possible live operation only after explicit validation and authorization
Mandatory controls

Safety before capability

  • Human authorization
  • Explicit operating modes
  • Exposure and loss limits
  • Order and position reconciliation
  • Idempotency
  • Complete audit trail
  • Versioned decision policies
  • Independent monitoring
  • Automatic stop conditions
  • Manual kill switch
  • Safe failure behavior
  • Separation of research, paper, sandbox, and live environments
Limits

No operational claim

  • VR4 is not currently operational.
  • No live autonomous execution is claimed.
  • No return, profitability, or performance guarantee is made.
  • Simulated results do not establish production readiness.
  • Prediction, authorization, decision, and execution remain separate responsibilities.
  • Live deployment would require technical, operational, legal, security, and risk review.
Current status · Long-term planned

VR4 remains long-term research and engineering. No production or autonomous live-trading availability is claimed.
