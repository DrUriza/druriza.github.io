Component architecture · gated modes
VR4 · DECIDE & EXECUTE

Risk-controlled decisions and execution

TradELATIN VR4 is the decision and execution component layer. It owns decision policy, risk controls, position sizing, execution modes, order lifecycle, reconciliation, exposure, limits and execution authorization. SIMULATION, PAPER and LIVE are VR4 execution modes; LIVE is explicitly controlled and gated.

No autonomous live-trading capability is currently claimed.

VR4 operating modes

SIMULATION · PAPER · LIVE

Modes share the decision and risk boundary; each transition requires explicit authorization.

SIMULATION

Deterministic scenarios and modeled execution frictions; no real orders.

PAPER

Paper orders and reconciliation without committing real capital.

Explicitly gated

LIVE

Requires authorized adapters, permissions, limits, independent validation and supervision. No deployment with real capital is claimed.

Authority boundary

LLM ≠ Trading Authority

VR5 can query, explain, coordinate and recommend. Execution authority, risk controls and execution-state transitions remain in VR4.

Prediction ≠ Execution Authorization

Human supervision and explicit VR4 authorization are required at sensitive boundaries. An orchestration request cannot bypass limits, permissions or operating-mode gates.

From validated output to audited responsibility

Main questions

What must override automation

  • When should a predictive output be rejected?
  • How should uncertainty affect exposure?
  • Which limits must override every automated decision?
  • How should costs, liquidity, latency, and partial fills affect execution?
  • How can every decision and order be reconstructed and audited?
  • Which tests and approvals are required before changing operating modes?
  • Under which conditions must the system stop automatically?
Component organization

Screen / Processing / Integration

Screen

VR4 Screen

Supervisory interface for decisions, authorized signals, orders, positions, exposure, limits, execution state, alerts, traceability, and kill-switch controls.

Processing

VR4 Processing

Decision policies, position sizing, portfolio constraints, exposure control, risk evaluation, execution planning, and safeguards.

Integration

VR4 Integration

Controlled connection of VR3 outputs with execution adapters, including permissions, idempotency, reconciliation, sandbox/live separation, observability, and end-to-end testing.

SIMULATION and PAPER model commissions, spreads, slippage, latency, fills, rejection, liquidity constraints, disconnections, stress and adverse scenarios within Processing / Integration. LIVE requires explicit authorization and separate deployment gates.

Safety progression

Deployment gates, not commitments

LIVE is a gated execution mode, not a claim of a real-capital deployment.

  1. Research only
  2. Historical replay
  3. Synthetic execution
  4. Paper trading
  5. Controlled sandbox integration
  6. Supervised limited deployment
  7. Possible live operation only after explicit validation and authorization
Mandatory controls

Safety before capability

  • Human authorization
  • Explicit operating modes
  • Exposure and loss limits
  • Order and position reconciliation
  • Idempotency
  • Complete audit trail
  • Versioned decision policies
  • Independent monitoring
  • Automatic stop conditions
  • Manual kill switch
  • Safe failure behavior
  • Separation of research, paper, sandbox, and live environments
Limits

Deployment and authority boundaries

  • Component architecture is distinct from deployment readiness.
  • No live autonomous execution is claimed.
  • No return, profitability, or performance guarantee is made.
  • Simulated results do not establish production readiness.
  • Prediction, authorization, decision, and execution remain separate responsibilities.
  • Live deployment would require technical, operational, legal, security, and risk review.
Current status · Component architecture / gated modes

VR4 retains decision and execution authority. SIMULATION, PAPER and LIVE remain distinct modes under explicit risk controls and authorization. No production or real-capital deployment is claimed.
