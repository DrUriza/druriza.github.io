Predictive component architecture
VR3 · PREDICT

Predictive modeling under temporal validation and uncertainty

TradELATIN VR3 is the deterministic predictive component layer for modeling and evaluating observations from VR1 and admissible structural representations from VR2. Its responsibilities include validation, walk-forward evaluation, uncertainty, calibration, explainability and versioned predictive outputs. Component validation and production deployment are separate maturity claims.

Prediction ≠ Execution Authorization. VR3 does not execute orders, hold trading authority or take direct control of brokers.

Main questions

What predictive evidence must answer

  • Does a feature provide information beyond simple baselines?
  • Is performance stable across time and market regimes?
  • Does the result survive transaction-cost and latency assumptions?
  • Is uncertainty calibrated?
  • Can the prediction be explained and traced to its inputs?
  • Does performance remain valid under distribution shifts?
  • Is the result statistically and economically meaningful?

Limits and relationships

Prediction remains separate from execution

VR3 may consume VR1 and evaluated VR2 outputs. VR4 consumption requires separate contracts and controls.

  • A prediction is not an order.
  • A probability is not a guarantee.
  • Backtesting is not live performance.
  • Predictive accuracy alone does not establish economic viability.
  • VR3 must not connect directly to live execution.
  • Use by VR4 requires explicit contracts, risk controls, authorization, and independent validation.
Current status · Predictive component architecture

VR3 is described at predictive-component scope, with deterministic processing and validation responsibilities. Release-specific validation reports must accompany claims about particular models. This website does not establish production availability or live predictive performance.
