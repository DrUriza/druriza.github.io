VR3 · PREDICT

Predictive research under temporal validation and uncertainty

TradELATIN VR3 is planned as a predictive-research layer that will evaluate whether observations from VR1 and structural representations from VR2 contain stable, measurable, and out-of-sample predictive information.

VR3 will evaluate predictions. It will not autonomously execute trades.

Layer purpose

From evidence to calibrated predictions

Inputs remain versioned and responsibilities remain separated from execution.

Main questions

What predictive evidence must answer

  • Does a feature provide information beyond simple baselines?
  • Is performance stable across time and market regimes?
  • Does the result survive transaction-cost and latency assumptions?
  • Is uncertainty calibrated?
  • Can the prediction be explained and traced to its inputs?
  • Does performance remain valid under distribution shifts?
  • Is the result statistically and economically meaningful?

Limits and relationships

Prediction remains separate from execution

VR3 may consume VR1 and evaluated VR2 outputs. Any future VR4 use requires separate contracts and controls.

  • A prediction is not an order.
  • A probability is not a guarantee.
  • Backtesting is not live performance.
  • Predictive accuracy alone does not establish economic viability.
  • VR3 must not connect directly to live execution.
  • Any future use by VR4 requires explicit contracts, risk controls, authorization, and independent validation.
Current status · Planned
